‘I have a knack for understanding people’, she explained. But I understand none as much you, she added to herself. How could she justify the hours that he takes up on her mind? How she watches him like she watches no other, listens like she hears no other. . . with habit, she had come to learn his rhythms and the curves of his mind. To know him and watch over him came as naturally to her as does to a mother for her child. With every new event that she perceived- the slightest swing in his gait, the gentlest bounce to his tone- she found the purest of joys reflected within her. It was a celestial connection, she said, one that no distance or time could sever.

She was a blip on his horizon. An interesting phenomenon, he thought, for something about her was off. He couldn’t quite place a finger on it. Was it how she never seemed to blink? Or how she always seemed to live an inch below your skin? A subliminal existence, most intriguing… and in a moment, the interesting phenomenon is brushed off his plane of consciousness- he had things to attend to, a life to live, a lover to caress.

Anon, the interesting phenomenon appears again, and he casts a curious eye in her direction. Sometimes, she elicits his chuckle, maybe a question or a remark in passing, then he returns to his day. She, however, will preserve and feed off this morsel that he has thrown her way for yet another forever, until she is visible again, a blip on his horizon. He sees her- almost, but not quite- subliminal at best.
