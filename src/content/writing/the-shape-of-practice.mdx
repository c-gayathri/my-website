## a small claim

Practice is not a schedule. It is the shape the day leaves behind — the
residue of attention, dried flat and kept.

Most days the shape is boring: a line, a margin, a note that goes nowhere.
Some days the shape argues with you. The work is showing up for both kinds.

## what counts

- the sketch nobody sees
- the paragraph cut twice
- the photograph taken on the way *from* somewhere

None of it counts, officially. All of it compounds, quietly, the way water
counts in a stone.

> Consistency is just curiosity that learned to keep time.
