  Chrysanthemums. Twenty today. No, thirty.
  Watchful chrysanthemums, yellow, aplenty.
  Sneering, leering, eyes searing into my skin.
  Yellow chrysanthemums - their death spangled on the floor.
  I take a step - hear a dried petal’s crunch - the silence breaks,
  Reminding me I was never alone.
  They come everyday, follow me around,
  Reminding me every waking moment,
  That I am watched, I am heard.
  So I measure my steps, measure my bared skin and I measure my breathe.
  At every turn my heartbeat quickens, and I walk faster,
  But the shadows follow me, growing large beside me.
  They’re black and yellow - the shadows and the light - the shifting shapes, and the shifting yellows.
  They come everyday, the shadows and the yellows -
  The stalkers and their yellow gifts.
