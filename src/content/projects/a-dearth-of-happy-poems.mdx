I write a poem.
Happy, pretty and sunny.
A poem that smiles
And takes you in its arms
Like a grandma in baking gloves.
I crumple it.
Toss it in a trashcan.

I write a poem.
Sad, tragic and painful.
A poem that bleeds,
Smells like a burning house,
And bites down like blades of broken ice.

I keep this poem.
I have earned it.
It fits my budget.
Doesn’t make me look stupid,
Like a snotty little kid,
That ran after butterflies
And fell in a puddle.
That licked cream out of orange cream biscuits,
Until he had an orange nose.

And his mom dragged him aside
And wiped the orange off.
And told him not to do it again.
To not lick out of cream biscuits again,
To not run after butterflies again.
Stupid little kid.
The little kid nods.
Sans an orange nose
And one less happy poem to write.
