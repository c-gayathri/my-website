A heavy summer afternoon. A heavy sultry air. And a heavy sun. So heavy, it heaves and labours in a sky blue liquid, unmoving like liquid mercury. The shadows evaporated and are vacationing in another hemisphere. The air is suspended in limbo, unmoving. Time warps and slows; drones on, lethargic.

Footsteps. A clink. A lush gurgle of liquid. An icy transparent liquid fills a crystal glass. Clear orange liquid. Ice cubes plop in. A citrus twang. Spears of light spike the crystal liquid with starry glint. Liquid touches tongue and the dense afternoon inertia breaks and shatters into infinite shards.
Infinite bursts of icy orange zest.
Infinite spears of citric light.

Is this what the stars taste like?
