summary: |
  I am hanging somewhere in the cornflower blue,
  Hanging in the whispers between azure and earth.
  I am hanging in the lime green stories they tell,
  Hanging, in the silences of their rustle,
  Hanging, eavesdropping, floating, suspended,
  I slowly dissolve, I disappear.
  And only the silence remains.
