Takes you on a journey along with Poonachi, a black goat who was born too tiny. 

You are instantly endeared to her; you laugh and weep and smile alongside her. Simple and honest in its narration, there is rawness in descriptions of sex and violence, while the tremulations of love and fear are captured with the tenderest care. It is interpersed with poetic turns of words and political commentary that arrives as matter-of-factly and without fuss as the clouds when Poonachi and her friends go out to graze.
